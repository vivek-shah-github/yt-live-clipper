How it works

Watch the 90-second tutorial, or read the three steps below.

  1. Paste the link

    Any YouTube live stream, past stream or video. On Android you can also tap Share in the YouTube app and pick YT Live Clipper.

  2. Mark the moments

    Watch the preview and press I where a clip starts and O where it ends, drag across the timeline, or type the times. Zoom in to land on the exact second, then press Preview to check it.

  3. Export

    Every clip is saved as an MP4 in your Videos folder (Movies/YT Clips on Android), named the way you named it.

Questions

Loading a link suddenly stopped working

YouTube changes often. Open Settings (gear icon) and press Update yt-dlp, then restart the app. The Android app also updates itself every couple of days.

The moment I want already happened in a live stream

Wait until the stream ends, then load the same link again. It becomes a past stream you can clip anywhere in.

The preview says the video can't play here

Some channels don't allow their videos to play inside other apps. You can still mark clips by dragging on the timeline or typing the times, and export works as usual.

Members-only or age-restricted streams

On Windows and Mac, open Settings and choose the browser you're signed in to YouTube with. Close that browser first if loading fails.

Android says the app could be harmful

That warning appears for any app installed from outside the Play Store. The full source code is on GitHub if you want to check it or build it yourself.

Can I clip anyone's stream?

Use it on your own streams or content you have permission to reuse.
